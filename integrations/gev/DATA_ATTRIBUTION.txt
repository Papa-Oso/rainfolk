Rainfolk adaptations: deterministic field validation, raw-value and provenance
retention, release-qualified identities, count/detail partitioning, and explicit
regional NAD83-to-WGS84 display transformations. Original NAD83 values and
quality flags remain in provenance. Invalid quantities are flagged, not guessed.
These are historical source records with incomplete coverage, unknown physical
well identity and unverified source location accuracy. They do not establish
current operation, drinking-water safety, ownership or absence of wells.

The citation year reflects the publisher's current citation, not a measurement
date or proof of data freshness. See per-source dates, release and file hashes.
