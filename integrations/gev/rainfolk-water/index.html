Legend, sources and limits

Hollow amber points are unverified source positions. Numbered circles group mapped source records; select a circle to zoom, or a dot or flag for source details. Counts at broad scales include whole intersecting aggregate cells.

Historical USGWD research compilation; incomplete coverage. Catalog records are not necessarily distinct physical wells or currently active wells. Original NAD83 coordinates are retained with explicit approximate display conversion; source location accuracy remains unknown. Invalid measurements are flagged without guessed corrections. No water-level observations or water-safety assessment are included. Nebraska and South Carolina remain separate acquisition blockers.

The bundled Natural Earth basemap is low resolution: it provides broad geographic context, not streets, property boundaries or site navigation.
